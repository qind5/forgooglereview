Everything you need to prepare

Winterview combines AI technology with proven interview frameworks to give you the practice you need to succeed.

🤖

AI-Driven Simulations

Practice with an AI interviewer that adapts to your responses, asks follow-up questions, and simulates real interview scenarios for your specific role and industry.

🎯

Role-Specific Questions

Upload a job description and your resume. Winterview generates tailored interview questions based on the actual requirements of the position you're targeting.

📊

Instant Feedback

Receive detailed feedback on your responses, including clarity, structure, relevance, and areas for improvement - so you can refine your answers before the real interview.

💬

Realistic Conversation

Engage in natural, conversational practice sessions that mirror the flow of a real interview - complete with follow-up questions and realistic dialogue.

Session History

Track your progress over time. Review past sessions, compare your responses, and see how your interview skills improve with each practice round.
