About Spreak

Spreak is an AI-powered interview preparation tool designed for job seekers in English-speaking markets. Our mission is to help non-native candidates practice and improve their interview performance through realistic, AI-driven simulations.

We are currently in development and have opened a waitlist for early access. If you'd like to be notified when we launch, please join our waitlist on the homepage.
